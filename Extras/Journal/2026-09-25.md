# 2026-09-25

- Step 5 of the validation plan: full validation in the background (`--background`, `--status`, `--wait`), released as 3.6.0.
- The background worker is the same `agt` binary with a hidden `--background-worker` flag, detached into its own process group with SIGHUP ignored, so it outlives the agent's shell. Its state is a small JSON record in `.build/agt/background/status.json`.
- Results are keyed by a working-tree fingerprint: the index copied to a temporary file, `git add --all`, `git write-tree`, plus the same tree for each dirty submodule. It covers untracked files, ignores ignored ones, and never touches the real index.
- Superseding is cooperative. A new run writes a fresh token into the status record; the worker checks it before each step and about twice a second during one, sends SIGINT to its current command, and exits. Killing the process group is only a fallback after 30 seconds: SwiftPM and `xcodebuild` put their children in other groups, so killing ours would leave them running, whereas an interrupted `xcodebuild` cleans up after itself. On ActionStatus, a foreground `--fast` stopped a background `xcodebuild` mid-build and finished in 18.5 s, with nothing left running.
- Bug found on the way: the worker inherits `--clean`, which removed `.build/agt` including its own status record. `--clean` now keeps `background/`.
- Known cosmetic issue: the step that was interrupted shows as FAIL in the superseded run's log.
